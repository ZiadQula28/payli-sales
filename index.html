[05] How Payli handles money

Payli is software. It never holds your money.

Guests pay through a licensed payment provider, and the money settles directly into the restaurant's own merchant account. The restaurant is the merchant of record. Payli sits beside the payment, never in it.

Card details never touch Payli

Guests enter card details on the payment provider's own secure page. Payli never sees or stores the full card number or security code, and the guest's bank can ask for 3-D Secure as usual.

Paid straight to you

Each payment settles to your own merchant account on your merchant agreement's schedule. Refunds are returned by your provider to the guest's original card. Payli never funds or holds a refund.

Your numbers, exact to the fils

Every bill, split and tip is recorded in a double-entry ledger, and exports as one clean file your accountant can use.

Honest about the beta: Payli itself hasn't yet been independently audited or certified, including against PCI DSS, and doesn't claim to be. Card data stays with the licensed provider.
