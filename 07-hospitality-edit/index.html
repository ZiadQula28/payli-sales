The last course is the bill.

Payli lets guests settle the bill from their own phone, at the table, with no app.

Settled at the table, on their own phone.

Guests scan the QR on the table and the itemised bill opens in their browser. They split it evenly, by item or any amount, add a tip and pay, in English or العربية.

Dinner ends when your guests are ready.

A live floor shows every table, and every payment is recorded to the last fils.

The money goes straight to your account.

A licensed provider takes each payment and settles it to your own merchant account; Payli never holds it, and there is no Payli fee on tips.
