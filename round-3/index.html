The same in all three

  • One set of verified facts. Nothing is claimed that Payli cannot show: no customers, testimonials, statistics, prices or partner logos.
  • One demo engine with two scenarios. Order Pager: the restaurant presses Start and Ready & ring, and the phone follows. Pay at Table: the guest splits, tips and pays, and the floor follows. It runs in the browser on sample data.
  • Honest status on every page: Pay at Table and the dashboard are in closed beta in Amman; the Order Pager is in development and not yet live at any venue.
