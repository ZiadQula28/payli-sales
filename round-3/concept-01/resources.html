Questions

If yours is not here, please ask it.

Money and trust

Does Payli hold our money?

Never. A licensed payment provider processes each payment and settles it directly into your own merchant account. Payli is software: it sends the bill, records the payment and posts the sale to your POS.

When do we receive our money?

From your payment provider, on the schedule set out in your merchant agreement. The money does not pass through Payli at any point.

How do refunds work?

Your team starts a refund in the dashboard, where it is recorded with who made it and why, and the provider returns the money to the original card. You remain the merchant of record; Payli never funds or holds a refund.

Is Payli PCI DSS certified?

No. Card details are entered on the payment provider’s own page and never reach Payli. Payli itself has not yet been independently audited or certified, and does not claim to be.

The product

Do guests need an app or an account?

No. The QR code on the table opens the page in the phone’s browser. There is nothing to download and nothing to sign up for.

How do guests split a bill?

Evenly, by item, or by any amount. Service charge and tax follow the items in proportion, nobody can pay twice for the same item, and the table stays open until the last share is paid.

Which cards and wallets are accepted?

Visa and Mastercard, and Apple Pay and Google Pay where your payment provider enables them. We confirm which is available for your venue in the walkthrough.

Is it in Arabic?

Every guest screen is in English and in Arabic, and the guest chooses. The restaurant dashboard is in English today.

Does it work with our POS?

Connectors for AQUA POS and Foodics are in development; Oracle MICROS Simphony and POSRocket are planned. Payli does not replace your POS: the bill comes from it, and the paid sale goes back to it. Please tell us what you run.

Beginning, and the Order Pager

Is Payli live anywhere?

Not yet. Payli is in closed beta in Amman and is choosing its first restaurants.

What does it cost?

There is no published price list. Pricing depends on the venue, and we take you through it in the walkthrough. Our fees are invoiced and never deducted, there is no fee on tips, and there is no hardware to buy.

What is the Order Pager?

The guest’s own phone replaces the buzzer: they scan a QR at the counter, follow the order and are alerted when it is ready, with no app, no account and no phone number. It is built and shown in demonstrations, and is not yet live at any venue.

Terms we use

Merchant of recordthe restaurant
The business in whose name a payment is taken. With Payli that is always your restaurant, and the money settles into your own merchant account.
Settlementfrom your provider
The arrival of the money in your account. It comes from your payment provider, on the schedule in your merchant agreement.
Reconciliationrun on demand
A three-way match between Payli’s ledger, the payment provider and the POS, so that the figures which should agree can be checked against one another.
Hosted payment pagethe provider’s own
The secure page, belonging to the payment provider, on which your guest enters card details. Those details never reach Payli.
