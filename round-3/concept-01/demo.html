What the demonstration shows

At the counter

Only your team moves an order: Start, then Ready & ring, then Collected. The guest’s phone follows each press at once, and the card reports back when the phone has received the alert and when the guest is on the way.

At the table

The guest opens the bill, chooses a share, adds a tip and pays. Table 12 moves from Bill open to Paying now to Paid, and the sale is posted to the POS only when the whole bill is settled.

What is simulated

Nothing here is connected to a restaurant. The payment provider’s page is a labelled placeholder, and no real notification is sent. In a walkthrough we show the same steps on your own phone.
