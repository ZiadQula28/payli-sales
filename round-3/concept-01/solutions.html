Pay at Table, the Restaurant Dashboard and the Order Pager.

Three pieces of software that share one guest web app and one dashboard. Each is listed below with its present status, and nothing is described as ready that is not.

  • Pay at Tableclosed beta
  • Restaurant Dashboardclosed beta
  • Order Pagerin development
  • Connectionsin development or planned

Pay at Table

In closed beta.

  1. 1. Scan

    One code for each table. The itemised bill opens in the phone’s browser: no app, no sign-up, no Wi-Fi password.

  2. 2. Split

    Evenly, by item, or any amount. Service charge and tax follow the items in proportion.

  3. 3. Tip

    Five per cent, ten per cent, a custom amount, or none. The full tip goes to the restaurant.

  4. 4. Pay

    Visa or Mastercard, and Apple Pay or Google Pay where the payment provider enables them.

For your guests

Nobody can pay twice for the same item, and the table stays open until the last share is paid. Card details are entered on the provider’s own secure page and never reach Payli.

Afterwards your guest sees a confirmation on screen, and may leave a rating of one to five stars with a comment.

For your team

Payli takes no fee on tips. Your team can view and print a receipt for any payment, and every guest screen is offered in English and in Arabic, with amounts in JOD to three decimals.

Cedar & Sage is a fictional venue used for illustration.

The Restaurant Dashboard

In closed beta. In English today.

One dashboard for the floor, the payments and the end-of-day figures. It runs in a web browser on any tablet, phone or laptop.

Each payment appears as it happens, with its table, bill, tip, total, method and time. A refund is started from the same screen and recorded with who made it and why, and tips are always shown apart from sales.

  • Live floorEmpty, Bill open, Paying now, Paid
  • Transactionsreceipts, refunds, a spreadsheet export
  • Ledgerdouble-entry, append-only, exact to the fils
  • Menuitem names in both languages
  • Ratingsstars and comments from your guests
  • Table QR codesone to print for each table
  • Reconciliationa three-way match, run on demand

The Order Pager

In development; not yet live at any venue.

The guest’s own phone replaces the buzzer. A guest scans a code at the counter, with no app, no account and no phone number, and the page shows your restaurant’s name and welcome line, the order number, a timeline and a time estimate.

When your team presses Ready & ring, the page reads “Your order is ready” and says where to collect it. It chimes if sound is on, and the guest may tap I’m on my way, which the counter sees.

It can be made your own: logo, colours, one of three themes, welcome line, pickup instructions and the wording of each status, in English and Arabic. Your team creates the order after taking payment as they do today, so it works beside any till.

The order page updates live, chimes when sound is on and, on Android, vibrates; with permission it also shows a notification. It follows the phone’s own sound settings. Ring the phone yourself in the demo

Connections

Your POS gives Payli the menu and prices, so the bill on the phone matches the one your team rang up. Payli sends your provider only the amount to charge; card details stay on the provider’s page. When the table is fully paid, the finished sale goes back into your POS with its items, tax, service, tip and tender.
