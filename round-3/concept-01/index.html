A good evening deserves a good ending.

Try the demo

Two short demonstrations, in your browser.

Payli is hospitality software built in Amman. It moves the bill, the split and the tip to your guest’s own phone, and keeps an exact record for your team. We are in closed beta.

See how the table pays

The last five minutes are the part your guests remember on the way out.

The meal is over and the table is ready to leave, yet the bill must still be asked for, printed, carried across the room and divided by hand, and the card machine is on another table. Each of those errands takes a member of your team off the floor at the busiest moment, and leaves your guests doing arithmetic.

Every guest already carries a better device than any restaurant could hand them. Payli puts the bill, the split and the tip on that device, with nothing to install, and gives the restaurant an exact record of every payment.

Pay at Table

In closed beta in Amman.

For your guests

They scan the code on the table, and the itemised bill opens in the phone’s browser with no app to download.

They may divide it evenly, by item or by any amount, add a tip or none, and pay by card on the payment provider’s own page.

For your team

The floor shows each table as Empty, Bill open, Paying now or Paid, and it is designed to be read from across the room.

Every payment is recorded with its table, tip, total and time, and only a fully paid bill is posted to your POS.

The money stays yours.

Payli is software, and it never holds your money. Your guest pays through a licensed payment provider, and the payment settles directly into your restaurant’s own merchant account.

Our own fees are invoiced separately and never deducted, and we take no fee on tips.
