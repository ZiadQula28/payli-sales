One moment, seen from both sides.

What your guest does and what your team sees, first at the table and then at the counter; where the money goes; and what is needed to begin.

At the table

Pay at Table, in closed beta. One table, from the first scan to the close.

The guestThe momentThe house

When the bill is wanted, your guest scans the code on the table. The itemised bill opens in the phone’s browser.

1 Scan

Bill open

Table 12 changes from Empty to Bill open on the floor. Nobody has been asked for anything.

The table chooses how to share: evenly, by item, or by an amount of its own.

2 Split

Bill open

There is nothing for your team to do: no bill run, and no arithmetic at the table.

Each guest adds a tip of 5%, 10% or a custom amount, or none at all, and sees the total before paying.

3 Tip

Bill open

The tip will be recorded on the payment and shown apart from the sale.

The guest pays by card on the payment provider’s own page, and a confirmation appears on screen.

4 Pay

Paying now

Table 12 shows Paying now. The payment then appears in Transactions with its table, bill, tip, total, method and time.

When the last share is paid the table is settled, and your guest may rate the visit from one to five stars.

5 After

Paid

Table 12 shows Paid, and only now is the finished sale posted to the POS. The rating lands in the dashboard.

Cedar & Sage is a fictional venue used for illustration. Try these five steps in the demo

Payli sits beside the payment, never in it.

Your guest pays through a licensed payment provider, and the money settles directly into the restaurant’s own merchant account. The restaurant is the merchant of record.

Payli sends the bill, records the payment and posts the sale to the POS. It never holds, pools or pays out funds.

A refund is started in the dashboard and returned by the provider to the original card. Payli never funds or holds a refund.

At the counter

The Order Pager is in development and not yet live at any venue.

  1. 1. Your team presses New order

    “We’ve got your order”

    The guest scans the code and sees an estimate.

  2. 2. Your team presses Start

    “We’re preparing your order”

    If it is running late, +5 min changes the estimate.

  3. 3. Your team presses Ready & ring

    “Your order is ready”

    The page chimes if sound is on. The guest taps I’m on my way.

  4. 4. Your team presses Collected

    “Enjoy your order”

    The order is closed on the board.

Only your team moves an order; nothing advances on a timer. If the guest does not come, your team can ring again after a short cooldown.

The board shows whether a guest has joined, whether the phone received the alert, and when the guest is on the way. It reports that the phone received the alert, never that someone heard it.

What you will need

  • A POS that Payli connects to, or one you would like us to connect.
  • A merchant account with a licensed payment provider.
  • Any tablet, phone or laptop with a browser.
  • A printed QR code for each table.

There is no hardware to buy and no published price list; pricing depends on the venue and is covered in the walkthrough.
