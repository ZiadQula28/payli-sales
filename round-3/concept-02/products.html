What each product does, and the state it is in.

Pay at Table and the Restaurant Dashboard are in closed beta. The Order Pager is in development. Every capability on this page carries its state.

Pay at TableClosed betaThe guest scans, splits, tips and pays at the table. Restaurant DashboardClosed betaThe floor, the payments and the record, in a browser. Order PagerIn developmentThe guest’s own phone replaces the buzzer.

Pay at Table

Closed beta

Four steps on the guest’s phone. Each step follows a rule the restaurant can rely on at the close.

Four steps. Swipe sideways to follow them.

1Scan

One QR code per table. The itemised bill opens in the phone’s browser.

Nothing to set up

No app, no sign-up, no Wi-Fi password. Amounts are in JOD, to three decimals.

2Split

Three ways to share: evenly, by item, or any amount.

Charges follow the items

Service charge and tax follow the items in proportion. Nobody can pay twice for the same item.

3Tip

5%, 10%, a custom amount, or none. The guest chooses.

No fee on tips

Payli takes no fee on tips. The full tip goes to the restaurant.

4Pay

Visa or Mastercard. Apple Pay or Google Pay where the payment provider enables them.

The provider’s own page

Card details are entered on the provider’s secure page. They never reach Payli.

After the payment

The guest sees a confirmation on screen. Staff can view and print a receipt. The guest can leave a rating, from one to five stars, and a comment. The table stays open until the last share is paid.

Cedar & Sage and Table 12 are sample data. Cedar & Sage is a fictional venue used for illustration.

Restaurant Dashboard

Closed beta

One login for the floor, the payments and the end-of-day numbers. It runs in a web browser on any tablet, phone or laptop.

  • My TablesAdd your tables. Print a QR code for each one.
  • MenuImport it from the POS where the integration supports it. Item names carry both languages. You control what guests see.
  • ReviewsGuest ratings and comments land here.
  • Export sheetExport the day as a spreadsheet (CSV).
  • RefundRefund from the same screen. The record keeps who refunded and why.
  • One row per paymentTable, bill, tip, total, method and time, as it happens. Print a receipt for any row.

Financial Transactions, as captured from the dashboard, with sample data. Cedar & Sage is a fictional venue used for illustration.

Live floor

Every table shows one of four states: Empty, Bill open, Paying now, Paid. The floor is designed to be read from across the room.

The Live floor with Table 12 paying now. Illustrative, with sample data.

Ledger

Double-entry and append-only. Exact to the fils. Tips are shown apart from sales.

Nothing is rounded to make a screen look tidy.

POS posting

Only a fully paid bill is posted to the POS. Partial payments never are.

A split bill reaches the POS once, when it is complete.

Reconciliation

A three-way match between Payli’s ledger, the payment provider and the POS.

Run on demand. It is not automatic.

Language

The dashboard is in English today. Guest screens are in English and Arabic.

English only, for now.

Order Pager

In development

The guest’s own phone replaces the buzzer. The Order Pager is built and shown in demos. It is not yet live at any venue.

  • Only staff move an orderNothing advances on a timer. Each change is a button a member of staff pressed.
  • It works beside any tillStaff create the order after taking payment, however they do that today.
  • One dashboard, one guest web appThe Order pager board lives in the same dashboard as the Live floor. The guest page is the same web app as Pay at Table.

Joining

Staff press New order, add what was ordered or leave it as a quick ticket, and show the guest the QR code. The guest scans it. No app, no account, no phone number.

The Order pager board with order #24 in Preparing. Illustrative, with sample data.

Estimates

A fixed range, a range computed from the items and the kitchen’s stations, or none. Staff can change it at any time: −5, +5 or +10 minutes.

If the time passes, the page says “Taking a little longer than expected”.

The page never invents a number.

Alerts

The order page updates live. It chimes when sound is on and, on Android, vibrates. With permission it also shows a notification. It follows the phone’s own sound settings.

The guest can tap I’m on my way. The counter sees it.

The guest’s phone: Your order is ready.

Ring again

If the guest does not come, staff press Ring again after a short cooldown.

The board shows whether a guest has joined, whether the phone received the alert, and On the way. It never reports that someone heard it.

Order #24 on the board: Ready, phone alerted.

Appearance

Logo, colours, one of three themes, a welcome line, pickup instructions and the wording of each status, in English and Arabic.

Starting templates exist for a coffee shop, fast food, a bakery, a food-court outlet and casual dining.

See the three themes on a phone

Connections

Payli sits beside the till and beside the payment. It connects to both and replaces neither.

Your POS gives Payli the menu and prices, so the bill on the phone matches the one your team rang up.

Payli sends your provider only the amount to charge. Card details stay on the provider’s page.

When the table is fully paid, the finished sale goes back into your POS with its items, tax, service, tip and tender.
