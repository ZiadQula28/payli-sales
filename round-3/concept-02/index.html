Your floor and your guests’ phones, in step.

Payli is hospitality software built in Amman. It moves the two slowest moments of eating out, waiting to pay and waiting to collect, to the guest’s own phone. The restaurant keeps an exact record of every one.

Two waits, one on each side.

The end of a meal and the wait at a counter are slow for the same reason. Each side is waiting on the other.

What the team waits on

  • The bill runThe bill is asked for, printed and carried to the table.
  • The terminal runThe card machine is on another table. A member of staff leaves the floor to fetch it.
  • The splitSix ways, by hand, at the table. Nobody trained for that arithmetic.
  • The closePayments, tips and POS sales are counted separately and matched by hand.

What the guest waits on

  • The billThe meal is over. The guest is ready to leave and cannot.
  • The card machineIt arrives when someone is free to bring it.
  • The orderAt the counter the guest stands and waits, without knowing how long it will take.
  • The buzzerOr the guest sits down with a plastic buzzer. Buzzers are hardware to buy, charge, clean and replace.

How the parts connect.

Read every drawing on this site the same way. The restaurant’s side is solid. The guest’s side is open. An orange line is an event that crosses from one to the other.

Your POS

Gives Payli the menu and prices, so the bill on the phone matches the one your team rang up. Receives the finished sale.

Restaurant Dashboard

Runs in a browser on any tablet, phone or laptop. Holds the Live floor, the Order pager board and Financial Transactions.

The guest’s phone

Opens the bill or the order page from a QR code. No app and no account. Every guest screen is in English and Arabic.

The payment provider

A licensed payment provider takes the card on its own page. The money settles directly into the restaurant’s own merchant account.
