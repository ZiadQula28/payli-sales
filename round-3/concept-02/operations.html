What your team does, and what it stops doing.

  • Before serviceThe menu and the tables are in the dashboard. Each table has its QR.
  • During serviceThe floor shows every table’s state. The team stays with guests.
  • At the counterOrders move across the board. Phones ring instead of buzzers. This part is in development.
  • At the closeTransactions export as a spreadsheet. Tips sit apart from sales. Only fully paid bills have reached the POS.

Table service

Pay at Table, closed beta

One table, five events. Read downward. The POS lane stays empty until the last one.

  1. scan

    1The guest scans

    The guest scans the QR on Table 12. On the floor, Table 12 shows Bill open. Nobody was asked for the bill.

  2. bill shown

    2The bill is shown

    The itemised bill opens on the phone. It matches the one your team rang up. Nobody prints it or carries it.

  3. paysshare paid

    3A share is paid

    One guest pays a share on the provider’s page. Table 12 shows Paying now. The payment appears in Financial Transactions with its table, bill, tip, total, method and time.

  4. payslast share paid

    4The last share is paid

    The table is settled. Table 12 shows Paid. The table stayed open until this moment.

  5. sale posted

    5The sale is posted

    Only now does the finished sale reach the POS. Partial payments never do.

Dashed strokes are money. They run from the guest to a licensed payment provider, which settles to the restaurant’s own merchant account. They never enter Payli’s lane.

Table 12 and its amounts are sample data. Cedar & Sage is a fictional venue used for illustration.

Counter service

Order Pager, in development

One order, from the counter to the hand-over. Staff press a button. The phone answers. The board records what the phone received, never what the guest heard.

  1. New order

    1The order is created

    Staff take payment as they do today, then press New order. They add the items or leave a quick ticket. The QR appears.

  2. scan

    2The guest scans

    The phone shows “We’ve got your order” and an estimate. The board shows that a guest has joined. The guest sits down.

  3. Start

    3Staff press Start

    The order moves from New to Preparing. The phone shows “We’re preparing your order”. If it runs late, staff press +5 min and the estimate changes.

  4. Ready & ring

    4Staff press Ready & ring

    The phone shows “Your order is ready” and where to collect it. The page chimes if sound is on. Nobody calls a number.

  5. alert received

    5The phone confirms

    The card shows that the phone received the alert. It does not say the guest heard it.

  6. Ring again

    Ring again

    Only if the guest does not come

    After a short cooldown, staff press Ring again. The phone is alerted once more.

  7. I’m on my way

    6The guest answers

    The guest taps I’m on my way. The card on the board shows On the way.

  8. Collected

    7Staff press Collected

    The order is handed over. The phone shows “Enjoy your order”. There is no buzzer to take back, charge or clean.

Order Pager is in development and not yet live at any venue. Only staff move an order. Nothing advances on a timer.

Order #24 is sample data. Cedar & Sage is a fictional venue used for illustration.

The close

Turn tables sooner. Close the day exact.

  • LedgerAppend-only and double-entry. Every amount is recorded in whole fils.
  • TipsA tip step sits on every payment. Tips are shown apart from sales. Payli takes no fee on them.
  • ExportThe day’s transactions export as a spreadsheet (CSV).
  • ReconciliationA three-way match between Payli’s ledger, the payment provider and the POS. Your team runs it on demand.
  • Your moneyIt settles to your own merchant account. Payli’s fees are invoiced, never deducted.

Five steps, in this order. We’re choosing a small first group of restaurants in Amman.

  1. 1See it on your phoneA 15-minute walkthrough of the guest’s side and yours.
  2. 2Check the fitYour POS, your payment provider, and what is ready for your venue.
  3. 3Set up your venueWe create your venue, connect your POS and bring in your menu and service charge.
  4. 4A QR on every tableAdd your tables in the dashboard and print a QR for each.
  5. 5Brief the teamThe floor board, receipts and refunds.

What you need

  • A POSOne Payli connects to, or one you would like us to connect.
  • A merchant accountWith a licensed payment provider.
  • A browserOn any tablet, phone or laptop.
  • A printed QR codeOne for each table.

There is no published price list. Pricing depends on the venue and is covered in the walkthrough. There is no hardware to buy. There is no fee on tips.
