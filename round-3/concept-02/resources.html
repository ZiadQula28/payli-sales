Questions

If yours is not here, ask us. We reply within one business day, in English or Arabic.

Payli and pricing

What is Payli?

Hospitality software built in Amman. Pay at Table lets guests scan, split, tip and pay from their phone. The dashboard gives the restaurant a live floor and an exact record. The Order Pager, in development, turns the guest’s phone into the pager.

Is it live anywhere?

Not yet. Payli is in closed beta in Amman and is choosing its first restaurants.

What does it cost?

There is no published price list. Pricing depends on the venue and we walk you through it. Fees are invoiced, never deducted. There is no fee on tips.

What hardware do we need?

None to buy. Guests use their phones, staff use any device with a browser, and you print a QR code for each table.

Guests

Do guests need an app or an account?

No. The QR code opens the page in the phone’s browser.

How do guests split a bill?

Evenly, by item, or any amount. Nobody can pay twice for the same item.

Which cards and wallets?

Visa and Mastercard; Apple Pay and Google Pay where the payment provider enables them.

Is it in Arabic?

Guest screens are in English and Arabic. The restaurant dashboard is in English today.

Money

Does Payli hold our money?

Never. A licensed provider processes each payment and settles it to your own merchant account. You receive it on the schedule in your merchant agreement.

Who gets the tips?

The restaurant, in full. Payli takes no fee on tips.

Is Payli PCI DSS certified?

No. Card details are entered on the provider’s page and never reach Payli. Payli itself has not been audited or certified and does not claim to be.

OperationsOrder Pager: in development

Does it work with our POS?

AQUA POS and Foodics are in development; Oracle MICROS Simphony and POSRocket are planned. Only a fully paid bill is posted to the POS. Tell us what you run.

How does the Order Pager alert the guest?

The order page chimes when sound is on and, on Android, vibrates; with permission it also shows a notification. It follows the phone’s own sound settings. No app and no phone number.

Do you support several branches?

Each venue is set up with its own menu, tables, team and settings. Tell us how your group is organised and we will show what is ready.
