Built in Amman for the last five minutes of dinner.

The end of a good meal is usually the worst part of it: waiting for the bill, waiting for the card machine, doing arithmetic at the table.

Payli fixes those five minutes, and gives the restaurant an exact record of every one of them.

The restaurant side

One browser dashboard for the floor, the payments and the end-of-day numbers. Staff see every table’s state and every payment as it happens. Only fully paid bills reach the POS.

The guest side

A QR code opens a page in the guest’s browser. No app, no account, no phone number. English or Arabic, chosen by the guest. At the table: scan, split, tip, pay. At the counter: follow the order and get an alert.

What we hold ourselves to

Four working principles. Each one can be checked on a screen in the demo.

Exact to the fils

Money is recorded in whole fils in a double-entry ledger. Nothing is rounded to make a screen look tidy. Tips are kept apart from sales.

Every amount on screen: JOD to three decimals

Both languages for every guest

No surprises at the table

What Payli is and is not

Payli is

  • Software for the table, the counter and the back office.
  • A record. Every payment is kept in whole fils, in an append-only ledger.
  • A link to your POS. The bill comes from it; the finished sale goes back to it.
  • A page in the guest’s browser, in English or Arabic.

Payli is not

  • A bank or a payment company. A licensed payment provider processes each payment.
  • A holder of your money. Payments settle to your own merchant account. You are the merchant of record.
  • A POS. It sits beside the till and does not replace it.
  • Certified. Payli has not yet been independently audited or certified, including against PCI DSS, and does not claim to be.

How it is built

Stated in plain terms. No figures.

Money in whole fils. Amounts are stored as whole fils, never as fractions of a dinar. A split always adds up to the bill: the last share takes the remainder.

An append-only ledger. Payments, tips and refunds are written once and never edited. A refund is a new entry, recorded with who made it and why.

Only fully paid bills reach the POS. Partial payments are never posted. When the last share is paid, the finished sale goes back with its items, tax, service, tip and tender.

Tested on every change. Every money path is covered by automated tests that run each time the software changes.

Each venue on its own. Each restaurant is set up with its own menu, tables, team and settings. Its data is kept separate from every other venue’s.
