        The full board

        How to read each side.

        What the restaurant side reports

        • Guest watchingA guest has joined the order on a phone.
        • Phone alertedThe phone received the alert. The board never says that someone heard it.
        • On the wayThe guest tapped I’m on my way.
        • Paying nowA guest at the table is paying.
        • Sale posted to the POSThe table is fully paid. Partial payments are never posted.

        What the guest’s phone shows

        • An estimate“Ready in about 8–12 min”. Staff can change it. The page never invents a number.
        • SoundOff until the guest turns it on. Turn it on to hear the chime.
        • The splitEvenly, by item or any amount. Service charge and tax follow the items.
        • The card stepSimulated here. In the product the payment provider’s own page opens.
