What this demo is, and what it is not

Everything above runs in your browser with sample data. The screens are the product’s own: the guest page in English and Arabic, the staff card, the whole board and the live floor. Only staff move an order. Nothing advances on a timer, so the phone changes when you say so.

It is not connected to a restaurant, a payment provider or a notification service. The payment step is a labelled stand-in, and the chime is a sound made by this page. To see your own menu on the phone, ask us for fifteen minutes.
