We’ve got your order.

Order Pager, in development.

You

You order and pay at the counter the way you always do. Then you scan a QR code and sit down wherever you like. No app, no account, no phone number. The page shows the restaurant’s name, your order number, a timeline and an estimate.

Meanwhile, at the counter

Staff pressed New order after taking payment, so it works beside any till. The card for order #24 reads Guest watching.

We’re preparing your order.

You

The timeline moves to Preparing while you stay in your seat. Turn on sound and notifications once, so the page can chime later. If the kitchen runs late, the estimate changes and is marked Updated. If the time passes, the page says “Taking a little longer than expected” and never invents a number.

Meanwhile, at the counter

Staff pressed Start. They can change the estimate at any time: −5, +5 or +10 minutes. Only staff move an order. Nothing advances on a timer.

Your order is ready.

You

The page says where to collect it and chimes if you turned sound on. On Android it also vibrates, and with your permission it shows a notification. It follows your phone’s own sound settings. You tap I’m on my way and get up.

Meanwhile, at the counter

Staff pressed Ready & ring. The board reports that your phone received the alert, never that you heard it. Then the card shows On the way. If you do not come, staff can Ring again after a short cooldown.

Enjoy your order.

You collect it at the counter. There is no buzzer to hand back.

Meanwhile, at the counter, staff pressed Collected and order #24 is done.

At the table, your phone is the bill.

Pay at Table, in closed beta.

Another day, another seat: this time you are served at a table. When you are ready to leave, you ask for nothing. Six frames, in order.

  1. 1 Scan

    One QR code on your table. The itemised bill opens in your phone’s browser. No app, no sign-up, no Wi-Fi password.

    Meanwhile, on the floor, Table 12 shows Bill open.

  2. 2 The bill

    Every item, with service charge and tax, in JOD to three decimals. You see exactly what you will pay before you pay it.

    Meanwhile, on the floor, there is nothing to do. Nobody prints or carries a bill.

  3. 3 Split

    Evenly, by item, or any amount. Service charge and tax follow the items in proportion, and nobody can pay twice for the same item.

    Meanwhile, on the floor, the table stays open until the last share is paid.

  4. 4 Tip

    5%, 10%, a custom amount, or none. Payli takes no fee on tips.

    Meanwhile, on the floor, the full tip goes to the restaurant, recorded apart from sales.

  5. 5 Pay

    Visa or Mastercard, and Apple Pay or Google Pay where the payment provider enables them, on the provider’s own secure page. Your card details never reach Payli.

    Meanwhile, on the floor, Table 12 shows Paying now.

  6. 6 Paid

    A confirmation on screen. Rate the visit from one to five stars if you like, add a comment, and leave when you choose.

    Meanwhile, on the floor, the payment is listed with its table, bill, tip, total, method and time. When the last share is in, Table 12 shows Paid.

Six moments of one bill, drawn by the product demo. Cedar & Sage is a fictional venue used for illustration.
