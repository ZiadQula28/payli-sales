The dashboard, in five lines.

Restaurant Dashboard, in closed beta.

One browser dashboard for the floor, the payments and the end-of-day numbers. It runs on any tablet, phone or laptop, and is in English today.

The live floor as Table 12 opens its bill, drawn by the product demo with sample data. Cedar & Sage is a fictional venue.

  • Live floor

    Every table shows one of four states: Empty, Bill open, Paying now or Paid. It is designed to be read from across the room.

  • Transactions

    Each payment appears as it happens. Print a receipt, refund from the same screen with who and why recorded, and export the day as a spreadsheet (CSV).

  • Ledger

    Double-entry and append-only, exact to the fils. Reconciliation is a three-way match with your payment provider and your POS, run on demand.

  • Menu

    Import it from your POS where the integration supports it. Item names in both languages. You control what guests see.

  • Ratings

    Guest ratings, one to five stars, and their comments land here after they pay.

Your money stays yours. Your POS stays your POS.

You are the merchant of record

Your guest pays through a licensed payment provider, and the money settles directly into your own merchant account, on the schedule in your merchant agreement. Payli’s own fees are invoiced separately, never deducted.

Refunds start in your dashboard and the provider returns them to the original card. Payli never funds or holds a refund. We confirm which provider is available for your venue in the walkthrough.

Payli does not replace your POS

Your POS gives Payli the menu and prices, so the bill on the phone matches the one your team rang up. Payli sends your provider only the amount to charge; card details stay on the provider’s page. When the table is fully paid, the finished sale goes back into your POS with its items, tax, service, tip and tender.

Only a fully paid bill is posted. Partial payments never are.

What you need

  • A POS Payli connects to, or one you would like us to connect.
  • A merchant account with a licensed payment provider.
  • Any tablet, phone or laptop with a browser.
  • A printed QR code for each table.

What it costs

There is no published price list. Pricing depends on the venue, and we walk you through it. There is no hardware to buy and no fee on tips.

Who it suits.

Table-service restaurants, casual to premium, get Pay at Table and the dashboard. In cafés with seating, guests pay from their seat, the queue at the till gets shorter and your team stays on the floor. Coffee shops, bakeries, fast food and food-court outlets are who the Order Pager is being built for.

For groups, each venue is set up with its own menu, tables, team and settings, and its data is kept separate from every other venue’s. Tell us how your group is organised and we will show what is ready.
