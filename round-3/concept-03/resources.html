Twelve questions, by who asks.

Guests ask

Do I need an app or an account?

No. The QR code opens the page in your phone’s browser.

How do we split a bill?

Evenly, by item, or any amount. Nobody can pay twice for the same item.

Which cards and wallets can I use?

Visa and Mastercard; Apple Pay and Google Pay where the payment provider enables them.

How does my phone tell me the order is ready?

With the Order Pager, in development, the order page chimes when sound is on and, on Android, vibrates; with permission it also shows a notification. It follows the phone’s own sound settings.

Restaurants ask

Does Payli hold our money?

Never. A licensed provider processes each payment and settles it to your own merchant account.

Who gets the tips?

The restaurant, in full. Payli takes no fee on tips.

Does it work with our POS?

AQUA POS and Foodics are in development; Oracle MICROS Simphony and POSRocket are planned. Tell us what you run.

What hardware do we need?

None to buy. Guests use their phones, staff use any device with a browser, and you print a QR for each table.

What does it cost?

There is no published price list. Pricing depends on the venue and we walk you through it. Fees are invoiced, never deducted, and there is no fee on tips.

Is Payli PCI DSS certified?

No. Card details are entered on the provider’s page and never reach Payli. Payli itself has not been audited or certified and does not claim to be.

About Payli

What is Payli?

Hospitality software built in Amman. Pay at Table lets guests scan, split, tip and pay from their phone; the dashboard gives the restaurant a live floor and an exact record. The Order Pager, in development, turns the guest’s phone into the pager.

Is it live anywhere?

Not yet. Payli is in closed beta in Amman and is choosing its first restaurants.
