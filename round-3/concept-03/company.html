Built in Amman for the last five minutes of dinner.

Who we are.

The end of a good meal is usually the worst part of it: waiting for the bill, waiting for the card machine, doing arithmetic at the table. Payli fixes those five minutes, and gives the restaurant an exact record of every one of them.

The same thinking now reaches the counter. The Order Pager, in development, moves the wait for an order from a plastic buzzer to the guest’s own phone. One company for both waits.

Payli is based in Amman and is operated by a company registered in the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan. It is built in English and Arabic, for how restaurants here work.

It is made for the guest first, and for the people behind the guest: owners, managers, floor and counter staff, and finance teams.

Payli is in closed beta in Amman, Jordan, and is talking to its first restaurants; no restaurant is live yet.

What we hold ourselves to.

Exact to the fils.

Money is recorded in whole fils in a double-entry ledger. Nothing is rounded to make a screen look tidy.

Both languages for every guest.

English and Arabic on every guest screen, chosen by the guest.

No surprises at the table.

Guests see exactly what they will pay before they pay it. Tips go to the restaurant in full.

Honest about the beta.

Payli is in closed beta. We say what works today and what is coming, on this site and in every demo.

Honest about what is ready.

Works today, in closed beta

  • Pay at Table: scan, split, tip and pay, in English and Arabic.
  • The Restaurant Dashboard: live floor, transactions, ledger, menu and ratings, in English.
  • Receipts, refunds and a spreadsheet export of the day.

In development

  • The Order Pager: built and shown in demos, not yet live at any venue.
  • POS connectors for AQUA POS and Foodics, tested against the vendors’ test systems.
  • Planned, not built yet: connectors for Oracle MICROS Simphony and POSRocket.
