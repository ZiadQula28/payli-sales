You wait for your order. Then you wait for the bill.

Payli moves both waits to the phone already in your hand, and gives the restaurant an exact record of each.

Two waits, and what they cost.

The wait for the bill

The meal is over and you are ready to leave. But the bill has to be asked for, printed, carried, split by hand and paid on a terminal that is on another table. The last five minutes are the least hospitable part of the evening, and they are the part you remember on the way out.

For the team, every bill run and terminal run takes someone off the floor at the busiest moment. At the close, payments, tips and POS sales are counted separately and reconciled by hand.

The wait at the counter

You have ordered and paid. Now you stand and wait, because you do not know how long it will take, or you sit down with a plastic buzzer.

Buzzers walk off, run flat and get sticky. They are hardware for the restaurant to buy, charge, clean and replace.

You already carry a better device than any restaurant could hand you. Payli puts the bill, the split, the tip and the order alert on it, with nothing to install.

Two products, one phone.

Pay at Table

In closed beta.

Scan the QR on your table and the itemised bill opens in your browser. Split it evenly, by item or by any amount. Tip 5%, 10%, your own amount, or nothing. Pay by Visa or Mastercard on the payment provider’s own page, then rate the visit if you like.

Try it at the table

Order Pager

In development.

Your own phone replaces the buzzer. Scan a QR at the counter, follow your order on a timeline, and get an alert when staff press Ready & ring. It is built and shown in demos, and is not yet live at any venue.

One screen for the restaurant.

Restaurant Dashboard, in closed beta.

While you pay, the restaurant reads one dashboard in a web browser, on any tablet, phone or laptop. The floor, the payments, the menu, your rating and a ledger exact to the fils sit in one place. The team can print a receipt or start a refund from the same screen. It is in English today.

See what a restaurant gets

The live floor after Table 12 has paid, drawn by the product demo with sample data.

“Payli is software. It never holds your money.”

That is what Payli tells every restaurant, and it shapes what happens when you pay. A licensed payment provider takes the payment on its own secure page and settles it directly into the restaurant’s own merchant account.

Payli sends the bill, records the payment and posts the finished sale to the POS. It never holds, pools or pays out funds, and your card details never reach it.

Payli itself hasn’t yet been independently audited or certified, including against PCI DSS, and doesn’t claim to be.
